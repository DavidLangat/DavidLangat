### Core Engineering Principles

- **Solve the business problem first** — choose the technology stack to serve the product, not vice-versa.
- **Boringly reliable systems win** — prioritize simplicity, deterministic execution, and end-to-end observability over unnecessary complexity.
- **UX is part of the architecture** — snappy response times and graceful error boundaries are structural requirements, not cosmetic details.
- **Leave codebases stronger** — champion deep modularity, automated test coverage, and documentation that empowers future engineers.
